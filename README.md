## How to download

Go to the [Release](https://github.com/JsuisSayker/Pikselite/releases) section of this repo, then click on the arrow next to **Assets**, and click on "PikseliteEngine-Setup-1.0.3-alpha.exe".
In your download, double click on the installer, and click on "yes" to allow the app to do modifications.

Then in the wizard window click "next", choose the folder path, and click "next", choose if you want a shortcut on your desktop and click "next".
Click on "Install".
Click on "Finish".
